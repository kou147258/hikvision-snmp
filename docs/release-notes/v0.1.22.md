# v0.1.22 — drop the 15 s `async_config_entry_first_refresh` wait

After v0.1.16 added the `wait_for(coordinator.async_config_entry_first_refresh(), timeout=15)`
guard, busy V5.x devices (active video streaming / recording) where
the SNMP daemon is starved by the video pipeline could take 15-30 s
for the first poll. The guard let entity registration continue after
15 s with `unavailable` state, but it still held the entity_platform
setup open for up to 15 s — which trips HA's built-in
"Setup of X platform is taking over 10 seconds" monitoring
threshold and surfaces two warning lines per setup event:

```
homeassistant.components.sensor: Setup of sensor platform hikvision_snmp is taking over 10 seconds.
homeassistant.components.binary_sensor: Setup of binary_sensor platform hikvision_snmp is taking over 10 seconds.
```

The v0.1.16 guard's 15 s cap was correct in principle (prevent
`CancelledError` from cancelling the entry setup), but the way it
prevented cancellation was to **wait for the slow refresh to finish
(or timeout)** — that wait itself was the source of the new
warning.

v0.1.22 removes the `wait_for` entirely. `async_setup_entry` returns
immediately, entities register in < 1 s, and the coordinator's normal
10 s poll cycle populates the data. The first poll still runs (in the
background as a `DataUpdateCoordinator` task), and entities that
reference its data will be `unavailable` for at most one poll interval
after entry setup — which is the same behaviour the v0.1.16 guard
provided, just without holding the setup open.

## Symptom

After v0.1.21, the user reported the following log lines on a busy
V5.x IPC (10.18.176.10):

```
custom_components.hikvision_snmp.sensor: Initial poll did not complete
    within 15 s for 10.18.176.10; sensors will become available once
    the coordinator recovers
custom_components.hikvision_snmp.binary_sensor: Initial poll did not
    complete within 15 s for 10.18.176.10; binary sensors will become
    available once the coordinator recovers
homeassistant.components.sensor: Setup of sensor platform hikvision_snmp
    is taking over 10 seconds.
homeassistant.components.binary_sensor: Setup of binary_sensor platform
    hikvision_snmp is taking over 10 seconds.
```

The first two are intentional (our guard fired). The last two are HA's
own warnings caused by the 15 s wait.

## Root cause

`sensor.py:async_setup_entry` (and the matching `binary_sensor.py`)
contained:

```python
try:
    await asyncio.wait_for(
        coordinator.async_config_entry_first_refresh(),
        timeout=15,
    )
except asyncio.TimeoutError:
    _LOGGER.warning("Initial poll did not complete within 15 s for %s", ...)
except Exception:
    raise
```

The `wait_for(15)` was correct in v0.1.16 to prevent `CancelledError`
from killing entry setup on busy devices. But HA's
`entity_platform._async_setup_platform` runs `async_setup_entry`
inside HA's own monitoring context that fires a "Setup of X platform
is taking over 10 seconds" warning at the 10 s mark. So the guard
prevented `CancelledError` but introduced the warning.

The right behaviour: don't wait at all. The coordinator's first
refresh is already running as a background task (started when
`DataUpdateCoordinator.__init__` schedules `async_config_entry_first_refresh`
in its `_async_refresh` machinery). The first refresh completing
just sets the data dict that the entity's `native_value` reads —
entities that are already registered will receive the data
automatically the next time they're queried.

## Fix

`sensor.py:async_setup_entry` and `binary_sensor.py:async_setup_entry`
no longer call `async_config_entry_first_refresh`. The setup function
returns immediately after building the entity list and calling
`async_add_entities(entities)`. The coordinator's first refresh
continues in the background, and the entity registry's
`CoordinatorEntity._handle_coordinator_update` method will populate
each entity's state once data is available.

The trade-off: per-channel and per-disk entities (which iterate over
`coordinator.data["channels"]` / `coordinator.data["disks"]`) need
data to be populated at setup time to be created. On busy devices
where the first poll exceeds 10 s, those entities are **not created
during the initial setup pass** — they appear after a reload
(when `coordinator.data` is already populated from a prior poll).

System OID sensors (model, CPU, memory, IP, etc.) don't depend on
data and are always created immediately, so the user sees the most
useful entities right away.

## What you should see

In HA on a busy V5.x device after upgrading to v0.1.22:

- **Before v0.1.22 (v0.1.21 state):** Setup takes 15 s on a busy device.
  HA logs two warnings ("Setup of X platform is taking over 10
  seconds") and our guard logs two more ("Initial poll did not
  complete within 15 s"). Entity setup completes at the 15 s
  mark with `unavailable` state, then data appears at 20-30 s
  when the first poll finishes.
- **After v0.1.22:** Setup completes in < 1 s. No HA warnings. No
  our-guard warnings. Entity registration is immediate; entities
  show `unavailable` for at most one poll interval (default 10 s)
  while the first poll completes in the background. Once the first
  poll finishes, system OID entities (CPU, memory, IP, etc.) start
  showing data.

## Edge cases

- **First poll fails** (e.g. network blip, device rebooting): the
  coordinator's `last_update_success` is False, system entities stay
  `unavailable`, and the next 10 s poll retries. No cancellation, no
  warning spam.
- **Per-channel / per-disk entities on busy devices**: not created
  during initial setup if the first poll exceeds 10 s. Two options:
  1. **Reload the integration** (Settings → Devices & Services →
     Hikvision SNMP → ⋯ → Reload) once the first poll completes —
     `coordinator.data` is now populated, so the per-channel entities
     are created.
  2. Wait for the first poll to complete and then re-add the entry.
  This is documented in the integration's Known Limitations and is
  the trade-off for fast entity registration.
- **HA restart on a busy device**: same as above — per-channel
  entities appear after the first successful poll + reload.

## Tests

Two test changes in `tests/test_sensor.py`:

- `test_sensor_setup_completes_when_first_refresh_is_fast` (v0.1.16)
  → `test_sensor_setup_does_not_wait_for_first_refresh` (v0.1.22).

  → `test_sensor_setup_completes_under_one_second_even_with_slow_first_refresh`.

  coordinator's first refresh is delayed by 5 s.
- `test_binary_sensor_setup_does_not_cancellederror_when_first_refresh_times_out`
  → `test_binary_sensor_setup_does_not_wait_for_first_refresh`.
  Same pattern for the binary_sensor platform.
